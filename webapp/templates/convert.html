How to prepare your vault

  1. Your vault needs Book Info.md (title, author and so on), Manuscript Reading Order.md and a Manuscript/ folder. New here? Download the starter vault and write in that.
  2. Not splitting your book into parts? Leave out the # Part heading in Manuscript Reading Order.md and list your chapters (## Chapter) directly. There will be no part page.
  3. Put a cover image inside the vault and set cover: in Book Info.md to its file name.
  4. Want your own title page? Add a Title Page note to Front Matter and list it first in the reading order (the starter vault has one to copy).
  5. Optional PDF and Word settings in Book Info.md: trim_size (e.g. 5.25in 8in) header_left and header_right.
