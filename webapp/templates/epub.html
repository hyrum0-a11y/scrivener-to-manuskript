How to prepare your vault

  1. Your vault needs Book Info.md (title, author and so on), Manuscript Reading Order.md and a Manuscript/ folder. New here? Download the starter vault and write in that.
  2. Put a cover image inside the vault and set cover: in Book Info.md to its file name.
  3. Zip the whole vault folder. On Windows, right-click it and choose Compress to ZIP; on a Mac, Compress.
